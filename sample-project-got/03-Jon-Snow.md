## Hook (2)
Jon as an outsider at Winterfell, yearning for purpose and belonging

1. The bastard's table - Feast for #robert_baratheon. Jon sits apart from his siblings. Watches #arya and #robb laugh. Talks with #tyrion about being a bastard. "All dwarfs are bastards in their father's eyes." #winterfell

2. Taking the black - Jon decides to join the #nights_watch. #benjen_stark warns him it's not what he thinks. Farewell to #robb and #arya. Can never have lands or children, but will find brothers. #winterfell #wall

## Inciting Incident (1)

3. Arrival at Castle Black - Jon arrives at #castle_black. Shocked by the quality of recruits - criminals and rapists, not noble rangers. Meets #samwell_tarly. Befriends the coward. Earns respect of #jeor_mormont. #wall

## Rising Action (4)

4. Training yard - Jon excels in training but alienates others. #alliser_thorne hates him. #tyrion advises: "Wear your bastard name like armor." Jon starts helping Sam and the others. Becomes a leader. #castle_black

5. Ghost finds the hand - Jon's #direwolf Ghost finds the severed hand of #othor, a ranger. Dead men beyond the Wall. Jon gives Sam his sword. The hand reanimates as a #wight. Burns in the Lord Commander's quarters. First proof of the threat. #castle_black #others

6. Choosing the Wall - Jon learns #robb is marching to war. Wants to join him. Duty vs family. #maester_aemon reveals he's #targaryen, gave up the throne. "Love is the death of duty." Jon chooses to stay. #castle_black

7. Ranging beyond the Wall - Jon joins the great ranging to find #benjen and learn about #mance_rayder's army. Heads into the #haunted_forest with Lord Commander Mormont. Snow is coming. The real war begins. #wall #ranging
