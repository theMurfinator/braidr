## Hook (2)
Introduce Dany as powerless exile, sold by her brother, setup for transformation

1. Pentos bathhouse - #viserys prepares Dany to meet #khal_drogo. She's terrified. He's cruel and controlling. "You don't want to wake the dragon." Reflects on childhood in exile. #pentos #illyrio

2. Meeting Drogo - Dany meets the #khal at a feast. He's intimidating but doesn't speak. Viserys sells her for an army. She receives dragon eggs from #illyrio - ancient, petrified, beautiful. #pentos #dothraki

## Inciting Incident (1)

3. The wedding night - Dany marries Drogo. Terrified of the wedding night. Drogo surprises her with gentleness. "No." First word of #dothraki she learns. Beginning of her transformation from pawn to queen. #dothraki_sea

## Rising Action (5)

4. Learning to ride - Dany learns #dothraki ways. Painful at first, but grows stronger. Drogo begins to respect her. She starts to love him. Viserys grows increasingly unhinged. #dothraki_sea

5. Discovering pregnancy - Dany realizes she's pregnant. #khal_drogo is overjoyed. Viserys demands his army. Threatens Dany and her unborn child. "A crown for a king." Molten gold. #vaes_dothrak

6. Drogo's wound - Drogo is wounded in a challenge. Dismisses it as nothing. Dany fears for him as infection sets in. Seeks help from #mirri_maz_duur, the #maegi. Desperate to save him. #dothraki_sea

7. Blood magic - Mirri performs dark ritual to save Drogo. Dany goes into labor during the ceremony. Loses her son #rhaego. Drogo is saved but left catatonic. "Only death can pay for life." #tent

8. Pyre - Dany builds Drogo's funeral pyre. Places the dragon eggs in the flames. Walks into the fire. "I am the blood of the dragon." Emerges unburnt with three living #dragons. #dothraki_sea #rebirth
