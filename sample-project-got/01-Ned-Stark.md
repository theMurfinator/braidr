## Hook (2)
Opening that establishes Ned's honor and duty, foreshadows danger from the North

1. Execution in the Wolfswood - Ned executes the deserter from the #nights_watch. His sons watch. Discussion of duty and honor. They find the #direwolves in the snow. #wolfswood #winterfell

2. King Robert arrives - The royal party arrives at #winterfell. Reunion between #robert_baratheon and Ned. Tension with #cersei_lannister. Robert asks Ned to be Hand of the King. #winterfell #politics

## Inciting Incident (1)

3. Jon Arryn's death - Robert reveals #jon_arryn was murdered. Asks Ned to come south and investigate. Catelyn urges caution. Ned accepts out of duty to Robert and the realm. #winterfell #conspiracy

## Rising Action (4)

4. Journey to King's Landing - Ned travels south with his daughters #sansa and #arya. Reflects on leaving #winterfell and #catelyn. Unease about southern politics. #kings_road

5. Small Council meeting - Ned's first meeting as Hand. Meets #petyr_baelish, #varys, #pycelle. Shocked by the crown's debt to the #lannisters. Realizes Robert is a poor king. #kings_landing #red_keep

6. Investigating Jon Arryn - Ned follows Jon Arryn's last steps. Visits the #armorer, learns about Robert's bastards. Discovers the truth about #joffrey's parentage. #kings_landing #conspiracy

7. Confrontation with Cersei - Ned warns #cersei_lannister he knows the truth about her children. Gives her a chance to flee. She refuses. "When you play the game of thrones, you win or you die." #red_keep #godswood
