MAYA · 1/HOOK

Maya intro in Brooklyn

The first drum rhythm stops Maya as she’s tying her shoes. On the second she reaches for her phone on the second, the third she sees her last text to her sister undelivered—See you at the bus! :-)— and on the fourth she mouths a prayer.

Once the drums start, they’ll keep their rhythm for hours or sometimes even days. There are night spent dreaming in drums, homework done to drums, dinners cooked and served to drums. It’s as if the drums have always been in Brooklyn and will always be in Brooklyn, even though Maya can remember just last summer when there were no drums. Ava would say that someday, when the revolution is complete and the world reborn, the drums will stop.

Maya checks her phone again. No service, but a text message from 0000:

A CELLULAR CURFEW IS IN AFFECT. DO NOT CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES.

Out of habit, Maya checks on Mama, careful not to step on the creaky spot on the floor, and seeing her deep in her chemical sleep, Maya climbs the two flights of stairs to the roof of their apartment building…
