# Braidr: Features & Benefits

## Core Concept: Plan → Braid → Write (All In One App)

Most writing tools force you to **choose**: outline OR write. Timeline OR character arcs. Scrivener OR Google Docs.

**Braidr does it all in one place.** Plan your story by character, organize the reading order, then write your draft - without ever switching apps.

---

## **The Three Modes**

### **1. PLAN Mode** (POV View)
Organize each character's story independently

### **2. BRAID Mode** (Timeline View)
Weave all POVs into reading order

### **3. WRITE Mode** (Integrated Editor)
Draft your scenes with full context

---

# 📋 PLAN: Organize By Character Arc

## **POV View** ⭐ (Unique to Braidr)

**What It Is:**
- See each character's story in their arc order
- Organize scenes into plot point sections (Hook, Setup, Midpoint, Climax, etc.)
- Scene cards with titles, descriptions, and expandable notes
- All scenes numbered in character arc order (Ned 1, Ned 2, Ned 3...)

**What You Can Do:**
- Add/delete/duplicate scenes
- Drag-and-drop within a character's arc
- Add section descriptions for story beats
- Collapse sections to focus on specific beats
- Toggle section headers on/off for clean view
- Expand/collapse all notes at once

**Benefits:**
✅ Plan each character's journey without losing the thread
✅ Follow proven story structure (Save The Cat, Hero's Journey, etc.)
✅ Focus on one POV at a time - no distractions
✅ See character arc progression at a glance

---

## **Scene Cards**

**Rich Planning Tools:**
- **Scene title** - Bold, auto-formatted
- **Scene description** - The "what happens" summary with inline tags
- **Notes section** - Expandable rich text editor with:
  - Bullet lists
  - Checkboxes for TODOs
  - Bold, italic formatting
  - Multiple paragraphs
- **Word count** - Track scene length
- **Scene connections** - Link related scenes across POVs
- **Metadata properties** - Custom fields (see below)

**Benefits:**
✅ All your scene planning in one place
✅ No more scattered notes across 5 apps
✅ Rich text means you can actually plan, not just list scenes

---

## **Smart Tagging System**

**Five Tag Categories:**
- **People** - #protagonist, #villain, #love_interest
- **Locations** - #castle, #forest, #spaceship
- **Arcs** - #romance_subplot, #mystery, #character_growth
- **Things** - #magic_sword, #mcguffin, #letter
- **Time** - #flashback, #present_day, #year_1453

**Filtering:**
- Filter any view by tag
- See all scenes with #protagonist
- Track subplot threads (#romance across all POVs)
- Find every scene at #castle

**Benefits:**
✅ Track characters, locations, and subplots without spreadsheets
✅ Never lose track of who appears where
✅ Verify subplot pacing (too long without #mystery clue?)

---

## **Custom Metadata Properties** (Like ClickUp)

**Add Custom Fields To Scenes:**
- **Text fields** - POV style, tone, setting details
- **Dropdown fields** - Status: Outlined/Drafted/Revised/Final
- **Multiselect fields** - Themes: betrayal, redemption, sacrifice
- **Color coding** - Visual organization
- **Fully customizable** - Add/edit/delete fields anytime

**Visible Everywhere:**
- POV view scene cards
- Braided timeline cards
- Editor properties panel

**Benefits:**
✅ Track status, tone, themes, conflicts - no external tools needed
✅ See metadata in context while planning AND writing
✅ Flexible system adapts to your workflow

# 🧵 BRAID: Organize By Reading Order

## **Braided View** ⭐ (Unique to Braidr)

**What It Is:**
- All scenes from all POVs in one chronological list
- Drag-and-drop to reorder the reading experience
- See the story as your reader will experience it
- Scene numbers stay tied to character arcs (Ned 3 is always Ned 3)

**What You Can Do:**
- Drag any scene to any position
- Insert chapter markers
- See character name + scene number for each card
- Filter by character or tag
- View unplaced scenes (not yet in timeline)

**Benefits:**
✅ Visualize pacing - is it 50 pages without your protagonist?
✅ Ensure reader engagement - strategic POV switching
✅ Experiment with reading order without breaking character arcs
✅ See the big picture - your entire novel in sequence

---

## **Rails View** (Visual Pacing)

**What It Is:**
- Each character gets a vertical "rail" (column)
- Scenes appear in their character's column
- Timeline flows top to bottom
- Optional: row height = word count

**What You Can Do:**
- Spot pacing issues instantly (long gap in Dany's rail?)
- Drag scenes between character rails
- See which character dominates each section
- Visualize story balance

**Benefits:**
✅ Film editor-style timeline view
✅ Catch pacing problems before drafting
✅ Ensure balanced POV distribution

---

## **Chapter Markers**

**What It Is:**
- Add chapter breaks anywhere in braided timeline
- Name chapters (optional)
- Visible in braided view and compiler

**Benefits:**
✅ Structure your reading experience
✅ Export with proper chapter divisions

✅ All context visible while drafting
